Tomorrow's Ontario power prices, with honest uncertainty

Every morning at 9:00 Toronto time, before the day-ahead market's 10:00 bid deadline, GridCast publishes a probabilistic forecast of the next day's 24 hourly day-ahead Ontario Zonal Prices, then scores itself against the actual prices.

How it works

  1. Collect. A server snapshots IESO's public market reports every hour and keeps every version, because most disappear after 30–90 days.
  2. Forecast at 9:00. Features use only information published before 9:00 the day before, enforced by automated leakage tests.
  3. Publish before 10:00. Each forecast is committed to a public, append-only log; it can't be edited afterwards.
  4. Score. Once IESO publishes the actual prices, the forecast is scored, good days and bad.

The model comparison and the research questions behind this are on the Research page.

A research project, not trading or financial advice. Forecasts are published as-is for transparency; no bids are placed.
